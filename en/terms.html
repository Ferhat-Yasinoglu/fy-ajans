1. What this website collects

  • This website is static: it has no server, no database and no user accounts. Nothing is sent to us when you visit the pages.
  • The contact and application forms open your own e-mail app when you submit; what you write reaches us only when you send that e-mail. You attach your CV yourself.
  • Course enrolment happens by e-mail. We use your name and e-mail address to issue the licence.
  • The student portal page is not yet connected to a working portal and does not ask for a password. Its «notify me» form, like every other form, only opens your e-mail app; your address reaches us only if you send the e-mail.

2. Cookies and browser storage

  • The website sets no cookies. There is no advertising and no third-party tracker.
  • The FYOS chat keeps its daily question counter only in your browser's storage; that number is not sent to us and disappears when you clear your browser data.
  • FYOS answers are generated on your device: on suitable devices, your browser downloads an open-source AI model once at your first question and computes later answers locally. Your questions and the answers are sent neither to us nor to any other server; the model cache disappears with your browser data.

3. External services and hosting

  • Every file of the website, including the font, is loaded from this website. The one exception is the FYOS chat: at your first question your browser downloads the AI library from cdn.jsdelivr.net and the model files from huggingface.co. This download starts only when you ask a question, and only the file request — never your question — goes to those addresses.
  • The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web server, GitHub may keep access logs; they are subject to GitHub's own privacy policy and are not accessible to us.
  • Your connection to the website is encrypted (HTTPS).

4. What we do with your details

  • Details you send us by e-mail are used only to provide the service you asked for.
  • Your details are never sold or passed on to any third party.
  • You can ask for the deletion of the details you sent us at any time by e-mail.

5. Conditions of the «AI Journey» course

  • The course licence is personal; it is issued to the buyer's name and e-mail address and embedded as a watermark in the videos.
  • Sharing, reselling, publishing or distributing the course content (video, files, text) is prohibited and will be pursued legally; the infringer's licence is cancelled without refund.
  • The course is paid in one payment; instalments are not available.
  • Course support is included for 45 days after enrolment; after that, an extension costs 10% of the course price.
